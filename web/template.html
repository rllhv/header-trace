How do I get an email's headers?
  • Gmail: open the message, choose the three-dot menu, then Show original.
  • Outlook: open the message, choose File, then Properties, and copy the Internet headers box.
  • Apple Mail: choose View, then Message, then All Headers.
  • Copy the whole text. Only the header lines at the top are read.

Headers show where a message came from and whether its sender was verified. They cannot judge the message itself, so links, attachments and wording still need your care.

Look-alike checks use a short built-in list of well-known names, so a clean result is not a guarantee.
